## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to the most common questions we get about the **tinyLeague™ ecosystem**.

---

### ⚙️ General Use

> **“Can I use the Founders Edition without a stand?”**

Yes — but the unit cannot sit flat on a table or floor, and sitting on a floor or table is far from optimal visibility and safety. We strongly recommend mounting it on a stand. Check out [our stand overview](/docs/shotclock-stands/introduction) for more info.

---

> **“How long does the battery in my module last on a full charge?”**

With a 10,000mAh battery bank, you can expect 8–12 hours of continuous use depending on the health of the battery and how frequent use is.

---

> **“Can I use any power bank?”**

Yes — any USB-A battery pack capable of 5V 2.4A output should work. Your battery may not fit in the holder, but this is not the end of the world. We recommend using a flat or compact model that fits securely inside the base housing area.

---

> **"Is the antenna required for use?"**

While you may be able use your unit without the antenna connected, we highly recommend you utilize it. It significantly increases the range and stability of your wireless connection.

> **"Do I need to update my unit when the app prompts me to?"**

Yes! OTA (Over-the-Air) updates ensure that your unit has the most up to date firmware. If your unit has out of date firmware, unpredictable errors may occur!

### 📶 Connectivity & App

> **“Do I need to pair the clock through my phone’s Bluetooth settings?”**

No. Always connect from within the **tinyLeague™ Hub app**, under **Settings → Available Devices**. Connecting from Android’s Bluetooth menu can cause unpredictable issues.

---

> **“Why can’t I see my device in the app?”**

Make sure the clock is powered on and showing the "scanning for connection" blue dot. Restart both the module and application.

---

> **“Is there an iOS version of the app?”**

Yes, the app is available on both Android and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tinyleague-hub/id6748661836)! Download the application [here](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tinyleague-hub/id6748661836).

---

### 🛠 Hardware & Setup


> **“Is the clock waterproof?”**

No — the Founders Edition is **not weatherproof** and should be kept dry. Outdoor use is possible under cover.

---

> **“What happens if the clock falls over?”**

We’ve designed the system to be durable, but a hard fall may damage internal components or the LED segments. Always test stand stability before live use. We always recommend using additional/extra weight on the base of the unit.

> **“Can I leave my Founders Edition overnight in my car?”**

While we have tried to build a durable product, DSS™ does not recommend leaving your shot clock modules overnight in your vehicle, especially during very cold weather.

---

> **“Does DSS provide warranty or repairs?”**

We offer limited support for Founders Edition units. Email [support@digitalsportssolutions.com](mailto:support@digitalsportssolutions.com) or reach out to a DSS staff member in the discord for specific repair or replacement questions.
