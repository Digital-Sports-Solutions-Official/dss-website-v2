## 📲 Installation Steps

1. **Open the Google Play Store** on your Android smart device.
2. **Click the badge above**, or search for ***tinyLeague Hub*** on the play store.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the download to complete.
4. You are ready to connect to your shotclock!

---

## 📱 Device Compatibility

- Requires **Android 11 (API level 30)** or higher.
- Optimized for phones and tablets.
- Recommended screen resolution: **720p and above**
