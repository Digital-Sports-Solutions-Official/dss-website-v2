### ✅ Recommended Stand

**Pros:**
- Minimal tools or skills required
- Lightweight and portable
- Clean, modern appearance
- Based on readily available parts + a simple 3D print

**Cons:**
- Slightly less stable than heavier builds
- Not ideal for permanent gym setups

### 🛠 Reliable Stand

**Pros:**
- Extremely stable and durable
- Uses hardware-store components
- Great for semi-permanent environments

**Cons:**
- Requires more tools and effort
- Heavier and harder to transport
- Less polished aesthetics

---

| Feature                     | Recommended Stand | Reliable Stand |
