## 🛠 Recommended Stand – Build Overview

The **Recommended Stand** is the most accessible way to mount your Founders Edition Shot Clock. It’s lightweight, stable, and requires minimal effort to assemble, with no workshop required.

---

### 🧩 What You'll Be Building

This build uses:

- A **umbrella weighted base** (to keep things stable)
- 2x **¾" 36" steel pipe** (threaded on both ends)
- A **¾" coupling fitting** (to connect your pipes)
- A **3D-printed TPU adapter** (to fit snugly into the base)

The end result is a modular, sturdy stand that’s easy to transport and customize.

:::info
This build is to create one stand, for one shot clocking unit. To get stands for both units in your set, you must build **two** of these stands.
:::

---

### 🛒 How It Comes Together

1. **The umbrella base** acts as the weighted foundation
2. **The pipe fits** directly into the stand base, and threads into the flange on the bottom of your unit.
3. A **single 3D printed part** helps secure the pipe into the stand base.

This setup requires **no power tools**, minimal assembly time, and can be sourced from Amazon, Home Depot, or Lowe’s.

:::tip
This is the recommended option because it balances portability, cost, and professionalism, without the need for cutting, drilling, or measuring.
:::
