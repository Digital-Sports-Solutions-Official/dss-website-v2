## 🛠 Step-by-Step Build Guide

This guide walks you through assembling the **Recommended Shot Clock Stand**. It’s quick, tool-free, and travel-friendly, so just gather your components and follow each step below.

---

### ✅ Step 1 – Gather Your Components

Make sure you have everything before starting:

- Umbrella base  
- ¾" TPU adapter (3D printed)  
- ¾" coupling fitting  
- 2x 3 ft (36") steel pipes
- Founders Edition shot clock module


**Be sure to 3D print your adapter using TPU filament.**

### 🧩 Step 2 – Insert TPU Adapter into Base

### 🔩 Step 3 – Attach Coupling to First 36" Pipe

Screw the ¾" coupling fitting onto one end of a 3-foot (36") steel pipe. Tighten by hand until snug.

### 🧱 Step 4 – Insert Pipe into Base

### 🔧 Step 5 – Attach the Other 36" Pipe to Coupler

### ✅ Final Check

- 🔩 Pipes are hand-tight and secure.
- ⚖️ Clock is upright and stable.
- 📏 Stand height is appropriate for visibility.
- 📱 Clock is ready to be powered and connected.

You're now ready to set up your **shot clock module**!
