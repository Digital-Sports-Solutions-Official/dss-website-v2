## 3D Printed Tabletop Stand

The **Tabletop Stand** is a compact, all-3D-printed alternative to the full pipe-based stand. It’s ideal for testing, personal use, or scenarios where portability and convenience matter most.

### 🔍 Why Choose a Tabletop Stand?

> **Pros:**
- 🧩 Fully 3D printable, no extra parts needed
- 🖥 Great for demos, desk setup, or tournament tables
- 💼 Ultra portable for training or backup use

> **Tradeoffs:**
- 👀 **Reduced visibility** compared to full-height stands
- 💥 No protection against impact on high-traffic surfaces.
- 🎯 Not ideal for mid-court or sideline mounting

If visibility and crowd awareness are key, check out the [Recommended Stand Build](/docs/shotclock-stands/stand-option-1/build-overview) instead.

**Print orientation:**
- Flat on the bottom

**Print Settings (Bambu Studio):**

- Generic PETG profile
- 0.4mm nozzle
- 0.28mm layer height
- 10% infill
- Grid infill pattern

**Print Settings (Cura):**

- Material: PETG
- 0.4mm nozzle
- 0.28mm layer height
- 10% infill
- Grid infill pattern
- No support
- Nozzle temp 255 oC
- Heat bed: 70oC
- 2 walls
- Print Speed: 40-60 mm/s
- Retraction Distance: 2-7mm
- Retraction Speed: 25-40 mm/s
