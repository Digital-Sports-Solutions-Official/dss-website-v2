## 🖨️ 3D Printed Parts

The tripod attachment for the standard ¾“ pipes requires multiple 3D printed parts. Most serve as fixtures to construct the tripod, but some serve as jigs to assist with measuring & drilling.

---

### 🔧 What 3D Printed parts are needed?

The tripod requires:

- 3x 3D-Printed Feet
- 3x 3D-Printed Tieoffs
- A 3D-Printed Gusset
- A 3D Printed Center Ring
- An ABS Pipe Drilling Jig

All of these files can be found on our [github](https://github.com/Digital-Sports-Solutions-Official/tinyLeague-files)

---

### 🖨️ 3D Printed Tripod Gusset

This gusset serves as the main attachment point for the 1-½” ABS piping to the standard ¾“ metal piping. The 3 spokes of the TPU gusset fit snugly in the inner diameter of the ABS piping, with a hole for using a screw to secure the connection. There is a slot for a thin ¼-20 nut to fit, allowing a short (less than ⅜”) machine screw to apply pressure, roughly fixing the gusset to the metal pipe.

### 🖨️ 3D Printed Tieoffs

These tie-offs allow for paracord to tieback the ABS pipe to the center pole, providing additional stability. These parts fit around the outer diameter of the 1-½“ inch ABS piping. The slots fit thin ¼-20 nuts, with the hole accommodating ¼-20 machine screws.

:::info
The model may also serve as a jig if you wish to drill mounting holes into the ABS piping.
:::

### 🖨️ PETG Pipe Jig

To properly affix the ABS legs to the TPU pipe gusset, holes must be drilled into the ABS pipe. To facilitate this, a jig has been designed to easily place a hole 1” down the ABS pipe.

:::caution
**Ensure the jig is printed in PETG filament**. Printing using TPU may alter the fit or location of drilled hole.
:::
