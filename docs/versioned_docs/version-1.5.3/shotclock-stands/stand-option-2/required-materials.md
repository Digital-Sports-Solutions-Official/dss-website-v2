## 🧰 Required Materials

To build the **Reliable Shot Clock Stand**, you’ll need several reliable components, as well as some tools.

DSS™ recommends you get your components at either **Lowe's** or **Home Depot**, but these parts should be available on Amazon or at any hardware store.

### 🖨️ 3D Prints for the Tripod

The **Tripod** requires various 3D prints. They are:

- 3x 3D-Printed TPU Feet
- 3x 3D-Printed TPU Pipe Tieoffs
- A 3D-Printed TPU Tripod Gusset
- A 3D-Printed TPU Center Ring

For a full breakdown of the necessary prints and to download available files, please see the [Reliable Stand 3D-Prints](/docs/shotclock-stands/stand-option-2/3d-prints) breakdown page.
